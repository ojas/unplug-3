# Discovery

*Working draft.*

Stripping away the noise, the part of coding I find seductive is the puzzle-solving.

There’s an idea. How can I make the machine do it? How do I explain it to the machine in a way it understands?

Often, you discover a pattern that’s completely unexpected. I once heard about the `3n+1` Collatz conjecture and became obsessed. Didn’t expect binary operations to come out of that.

A reference to return to: [Dylan Beattie on discovery and the Mandelbrot set](https://youtu.be/6avJHaC3C2U?si=ANXv9cyiwrLRnVzY&t=944).

One of my favorite words is “Discovery.” My favorite watch is a Hamilton with “Eureka” in Morse code on the second hand. I got chills thinking about the connection.

I learn of musicians, artists, athletes, free spirits, inventors, scientists, mathematicians. Even young YouTubers sharing how to make music using some esoteric software library. I’m curious, and I often take some action.

I certainly don’t complete everything. The RX7 EV project. The Auto Dojo container home. The times I pick up an instrument and don’t master it.

Lately, I like a good story. Always writing a better or more interesting one.
