# What I did with the time

I keep a running file called “Sabbatical Wins.” I add to it as I go: things completed, experiences worth remembering, practices sustained, and ideas taking shape.

Keeping that record becomes especially important when I have more free time. Without the usual structure of work, it’s easy to lose sight of where the time went. This file helps me notice—and remember.

Some of the time went toward closing long-running obligations. Some went toward family, travel, and daily practice. Some went into making things—software, music, photographs, spaces, and an archive of my father’s work.

Here is a working inventory of what that looked like.

## Making room

- **OP, LLC:** Sold three properties, two involving renovations. Worked through difficult market conditions and problems with service providers. One sale provided a soft landing.
- **Village Sanctuary / Miles property:** Developed a transition plan involving short- and medium-term rentals, renovation, preservation, and support for a wellness community. An ambitious, challenging undertaking that required stepping up to the plate.

- An LFA cruise into the sunset with Jak.
