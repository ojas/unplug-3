# A practice of taking time off

I've had an engaging career building software, shaping products, and working with people who care about what they make. Along the way, I've taken regular breaks.

Over time, taking time off has become a practice: making room to step back, follow curiosity, and consider how I want to spend the next stretch of my life.

This is where I want to tell the story of how I came to adopt that practice—what prompted the breaks, what I discovered in them, and how those experiences shaped my relationship with work.

The current sabbatical is the latest chapter. These pages are a record in progress: experiences, experiments, maintenance, and questions I'm still living with.
