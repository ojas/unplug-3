## Leaving space for grace

Give the day some room. An encounter, a detour, something I wouldn’t have thought to put on the calendar. I’m getting better at leaving space for what might show up.

[Yoyu](japan.md#yoyu). Having an abundance of space in your heart. I experienced this in Japan, on a delayed bus en route to Hakone. A story to return to.

## Shifting obsession into devotion

Care for the thing. Give myself to the practice. There’s something here about where I place my attention, and what that attention becomes.

I’ve been exploring this in [Ikigai](https://ojas.net/ikigai), which also embeds the [Field of Attention](https://cx22.ns-x.net/cygnus_x1/labs/VIZ-01/field/index).

## Making things just for fun

Recreational programming. A little experiment. Something I’m curious about and want to see working.

Sometimes “I wonder…” is enough to get started.

A reference to return to: [Alex Zanardi on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DX2G29AkeOx/).

## Being a beginner again

Math, music, Lego. Following a question, working out how something works, getting a little further than I could yesterday.

There’s pleasure in learning itself. I want to keep making time for that.

## Letting myself contain multitudes

Engineer, musician, car enthusiast, learner. These parts of my life can have room to breathe. I can spend more time with one for a while and return to another.

Brad Stulberg describes an [identity with multiple rooms](https://thegrowtheq.com/rugged-flexibility-and-diversifying-your-sense-of-identity/). When one room is disrupted, the others give us somewhere to stand. That’s an idea worth keeping close as I figure out what comes next.
