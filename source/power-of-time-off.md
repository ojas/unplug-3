## On time to do nothing

* We rarely have a chance to stop and just think.
* We often think of the opportunity cost of not doing something, but rarely the opportunity cost of doing something.
* [The Power of Time Off by Stefan Sagmeister](http://www.ted.com/talks/stefan_sagmeister_the_power_of_time_off.html) - Golden era of TED Talks.
* Don't underestimate the value of Doing Nothing, of just going along, listening to all the things you can't hear, and not bothering.
* The thing that I should wish to obtain from money would be leisure with security.
* Idleness is not just a vacation, an indulgence or a vice; it is as indispensable to the brain as vitamin D is to the body, and deprived of it we suffer a mental affliction as disfiguring as rickets.
* Il Dolce Far Niente ("The Sweetness of Doing Nothing")
* Usefulness of Useless Knowledge <http://www.brainpickings.org/index.php/2012/07/27/the-usefulness-of-useless-knowledge/>
