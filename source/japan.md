# Japan

*As a traveller, not a tourist.*

In my travel journal, I wrote:

> Japan has self-respect. Invests in its children, its future. Not an accident. Does the hard things. Pursues the impossible dream.

This is an impression I want to explore through the experiences behind it. What did I see that led me to write those words? What could I learn by paying closer attention?

## Threads to develop

- The encounters and places that prompted this journal entry.
- Craftsmanship, care, and the standards people bring to their work.
- Motoring heritage, factories, and the pursuit of difficult dreams.
- What it means to invest in children and the future.
- Where my impressions were challenged or complicated.
- What I might bring into my own way of living.

*Working notes — the stories behind the observation are still to come.*
